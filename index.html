About Me

I am an upcoming graduate of Computer Science & Engineering, and I have a keen interest in studying intelligent system solutions for real-world applications. The challenge of information security intrigues me because of its ability to protect data silently and uncover threats that lie openly before us. This intruiguing aspect has led me to publish two research papers in QPAIN 2026. Currently, I am finishing up my undergraduate thesis titled "HealGNN: Predictive Disease Risk Modeling from Environmental Factors Using Spatiotemporal Graph Neural Networks and Counterfactual Goal Directed Policy Interventions". This research is inspired by my interest in the applications of graph neural networks in public health and policy simulations. In completing my graduation degree, I will be looking forward to continue learning more.
